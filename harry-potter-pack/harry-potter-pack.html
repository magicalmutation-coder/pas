Harry Potter Personality Pack

160 named characters from the books and films, written as working voices for the agent, plus a Shakespearean insult-and-curse voice. Pick one here as the default for new runs, or choose it per conversation in the Chat bar. The prompt behind every voice is editable in Settings → Personalities.

current: -

The avatars are original drawings made for this pack, not stills. They land in the avatar picker when the pack is installed, so a crew bot can wear them too. A voice only changes how a run writes; the tools, the approval rules and the workspace stay the same. The pack is data: personality-packs\*.json, and the marker in jobs\personality-packs.json means a character you delete stays deleted.
