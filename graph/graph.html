Connect Microsoft 365

Sign in once with your Microsoft account. The app keeps a refresh token, encrypted with your Windows account, in jobs\graph-tokens.json. Nothing else leaves this machine except the Graph calls you ask for.

The client id and secret live in Settings > Microsoft 365, and the Tenant there must be your tenant id - not common - because the app registration is single-tenant.

Add this Redirect URI to the app registration (Authentication > Web): http://localhost:7796/api/modules/graph/callback
